DIFFERENT PURPOSES

Degree Verification vs Degree Apostille

Many applicants confuse academic verification with apostille or legalization. They serve different purposes.

Degree Verification

  • May confirm academic information through the relevant verification platform, issuing university or other source specified by the recipient.
  • Often used for immigration, WES, ECA, employment and university admissions.
  • May involve a report from the relevant Chinese verification platform, university confirmation or a credential evaluation agency specified by the recipient.

Degree Apostille / Legalization

  • Confirms the authenticity of the document itself for overseas use.
  • Often required by foreign governments, employers or licensing bodies.
  • May involve notarization, apostille or consular legalization.

An apostille authenticates the relevant official signature, seal or capacity; it does not certify academic equivalency or the truth of the underlying record.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Frequently Asked Questions

Start with the report and route your recipient requires.

Q: Does degree verification always have to be done in the country where I studied?

A: Not always. Some routes may be available online or from overseas, but this depends on the issuing institution, verification platform, receiving organization and authorization requirements.

We can help identify the available route based on where you are now and where the Chinese qualification will be used.

Q: Can I reuse my verification report multiple times?

A: Possibly. Reuse depends on the report type, its current online-verification status or validity information, and the receiving organization's own recency and delivery rules. Check the report and the recipient's current instructions before relying on a previously issued copy.

Q: I am still studying. Is it useful to plan ahead now?

A: Yes. The earlier you understand the requirements of your target country or institution, the easier it is to avoid issues with course selection, language scores and internship arrangements later on.

Q: What is China degree verification?

A: China degree verification is the process of confirming that a degree, graduation certificate, transcript or other academic record is genuine and matches the issuing institution's records.

The appropriate route may involve a Chinese education verification platform, direct university confirmation or an overseas credential evaluation provider, depending on the receiving authority. Verification is commonly used for international study, employment, immigration and professional licensing.

It is different from notarization or apostille, which authenticate documents for legal use rather than assess the academic qualification itself.

Q: Can I verify both my degree certificate and transcript?

A: A receiving organization may request both records, but degree and transcript verification are separate and may require different reports, document uploads, translations or university checks. Some applications need only evidence of the qualification, while a course-by-course evaluation may require a complete transcript.

Before submitting, confirm the recipient's checklist, required language, delivery method and whether results must be sent through a specified platform or by the institution.

Q: Can I apply for degree verification from overseas?

A: Some China degree verification cases can be prepared from overseas using available academic records, identification documents and online services. The available route depends on the university, graduation year, verification platform and receiving organization's requirements.

A case may require authorization, original documents, certified translations or direct confirmation from the Chinese institution. Applicants should check whether reports must be delivered electronically, mailed in a sealed envelope or sent directly to an evaluator.

Incomplete transcripts, changed passport details or older school records can extend the review.

Q: How long does China degree verification take?

A: There is no single processing period for every case. Timing depends on university response, record completeness, graduation year, degree level, the relevant verification platform, report type, translation needs and whether additional records are requested.

Older qualifications, inconsistent personal details, school holidays or direct institutional confirmation can cause delays. WES or another credential evaluation, notarization, apostille and courier delivery are separate stages that may add time.

Check the current status of the relevant organizations and the receiving authority's deadline before starting.

Can Chinese degrees be used for Canada immigration?

A Chinese degree may be assessed through WES or another ECA organization when required by the applicable immigration program. Follow the current program and evaluator instructions.

Can I use my Chinese degree in the USA?

A U.S. university, employer or licensing body may request a credential evaluation or other academic evidence. The recipient decides the accepted evaluator and document route.

What is the difference between WES and Chinese academic verification?

CHSI/CSSD reports or university confirmation may verify specified Chinese academic information, while WES independently evaluates submitted credentials under its own requirements.

Can verified degrees also be apostilled?

Where the destination requires it, a qualifying academic or notarial document may proceed through a separate apostille or legalization route. Verification does not automatically make a document eligible or accepted.

Is CHSI the same as CSSD for China degree verification?

CHSI and CSSD are commonly associated with China higher education verification routes. The required report format depends on whether the receiving authority asks for degree verification, transcript verification or both.

Do I need transcript verification as well as degree verification?

The receiving organization may request one or both. A course-by-course evaluation may require transcript evidence, but applicants should follow the exact checklist for their case.

Can China degree verification be used for WES?

China degree and transcript verification can be part of the WES preparation route when WES requests verified Chinese academic records or supporting education reports.

Can verified China academic documents be apostilled?

Depending on the destination and document form, a qualifying academic or notarial document may proceed to Hague apostille or consular legalization. The competent authority and final recipient determine the applicable requirements.

Which report types are available?

Degree, qualification, student-record and transcript reports have different purposes. The available route depends on the academic record and recipient’s exact request; other institutions or evaluators may require separate services.

Can you help with CHSI account registration?

We can review the available assistance where applicable. Account registration and identity checks remain subject to the platform’s current procedures, and an account does not guarantee that a particular academic record can be matched.

What if my old passport details differ from my current passport?

Confirm with your university which identifier was used in the academic record. Evidence of the earlier and current identity details may be relevant, depending on the platform and institution. Do not create an application using an assumed record identifier.

What if my university record is missing or incorrect?

The university’s responsible records department may need to check and correct the source information. The available procedure and review time depend on the institution and platform; a correction cannot be guaranteed.
