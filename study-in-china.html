Frequently Asked Questions

What programs can I apply for in China?⌄

Options may include bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral degrees, Chinese language courses, and exchange or short-term study where available. We review your education, language level and goals against each institution’s current requirements. English-taught provision varies by university and subject.

What documents do I need to provide?⌄

Universities may request your passport details, academic certificates and transcripts, language results, a personal statement or study plan, CV and recommendation letters. The exact list, format and any additional requirements depend on the university, program, nationality and application cycle. Share your background first; we will help organize a relevant checklist.

Do I need to take HSK or IELTS?⌄

Chinese-taught degrees often require HSK; English-taught programs may ask for IELTS, TOEFL or other evidence accepted by the university. Exemptions and language preparation routes vary. Check the current program instructions before booking a test.

How much does it cost to study in China?⌄

Tuition, accommodation, insurance and everyday costs vary by university, subject, city and lifestyle. Ask your target university for current fees and check the precise coverage of any scholarship. Our assistance fee is separate and depends on the agreed service scope and number of applications; a quotation is provided before proceeding.

When should I start the application process?⌄

The existing service guidance recommends beginning 6–12 months ahead, and earlier for scholarships, to allow for language tests, program selection and document preparation. This is a planning recommendation, not a fixed deadline. Confirm the application cycle and scholarship dates with each university.

Can you help with the student visa (X1/X2) application?⌄

We can explain the document preparation steps after admission and provide practical checklists. Use the current instructions of the Chinese embassy, consulate or visa centre responsible for your application. The required route and documents depend on your study duration and circumstances. Visa decisions remain with the authorities; we do not guarantee approval.

Original planning questions & arrival support
How early should I start preparing for China student document applications?⌄

We recommend starting at least 6–12 months in advance (earlier for scholarships). Early planning allows time for language tests, school selection, document preparation, and deadlines.

Do I need Chinese language ability to study in China?⌄

It depends on the program type. Chinese-taught degrees often require HSK. English-taught programs may focus on IELTS/TOEFL. We help you choose based on your level and targets.

How much are tuition and living costs in China?⌄

Costs vary by city and university. Many bachelor’s and master’s programs range roughly from RMB 15,000–50,000/year, with living costs varying by location and lifestyle.

What should I pay attention to for student visas (X1/X2)?⌄

Ensure admission and JW form details match your passport, prepare required documents, and complete residence permit plus local registration after arrival within the specified timeframe.

Do you support students after they arrive in China?⌄

Yes. Based on your needs, we can guide onboarding steps (registration, housing, insurance) and assist with school communication when needed.

The tuition range in this retained guidance is an indicative existing website estimate, not a current university quotation or a fee guarantee. Confirm current fees directly with the university. Visa instructions and available support depend on your circumstances.
