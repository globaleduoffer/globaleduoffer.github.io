Overview

Do You Need a China PCC for Germany?

A German authority, employer or institution may ask for a China Police Certificate as evidence of your criminal-record history in China. Whether it is needed depends on the application, your circumstances and the actual recipient’s instructions. It is not a universal requirement for every German visa, residence or employment application.

Start with the German missions’ visa guidance and your specific checklist. For a concrete professional example, Bavaria’s published China nursing-recognition checklist requests a Chinese criminal-record certificate at a later stage for applicants not yet living in Germany. That is a particular recognition procedure, not a rule for every profession or visa.

The China-side issuance process and the German recipient’s submission requirements are separate. A German Führungszeugnis issued by the Federal Office of Justice is a different document from a China PCC; one is not automatically a substitute for the other.

See our China Police Certificate Service for China-side support. Global Attest is an independent document assistance provider, not a Chinese issuing authority or a German government body.

Document Preparation

Required Documents: Start With Your China Records

This is a preparation guide, not a nationwide mandatory checklist. Requirements depend on nationality, city, residence period and the local authority’s review.

Identity & Travel History

  • Current passport bio page.
  • Former passport used during your stay in China, if available.
  • Chinese visas, entry/exit stamps and residence permits, as applicable.
  • Evidence explaining a name change or passport-number mismatch.

Residence & Authorization

  • Temporary Residence Registration Form, where applicable.
  • Dates and addresses in each Chinese city.
  • Work or school records that may help locate archives.
  • Power of attorney or other authorization, where permitted.
  • Photos or fingerprints only if the local procedure requests them.

This is not a nationwide mandatory list. Chinese citizens may also need identity, hukou and local records; foreign applicants follow the relevant local procedure. Bring the German request letter and any appointment or submission deadline to the route review. Additional application forms, photographs or fingerprints are required only where the local authority requests them. We cannot guarantee document recovery or certificate issuance.

Applying From Outside China

How to Get a China PCC for Germany After Leaving China

Confirm the Local Application Route

Having left China does not automatically rule out an application. Tell us your former Chinese cities, residence dates, current country, passport history and available records. Online or authorized representative applications may be possible, but only where the responsible local authority permits them.

Old passports, Chinese visas, residence permits and accommodation registration can affect eligibility and record checks. A visitor visa and a residence permit are not interchangeable. Some routes require personal attendance, additional identity checks or locally specified authorization.

Missing Records or an Approaching Deadline?

For a changed or lost passport, missing registration, several Chinese cities or old school and employment records, we first review what can be verified locally. Where procedures allow, we can ask about archived or reissued records; availability is not assured.

Send the German recipient’s exact request and appointment date before ordering authorization, translation or courier services. If the certificate cannot be issued, ask that recipient which explanation or alternative evidence it will consider. There is no automatic substitute or guaranteed waiver.

Germany Document Format

Does a China Police Certificate for Germany Need Translation or an Apostille?

Confirm the exact document chain before commissioning extra services. A police-issued record, a Chinese notarial certificate, a translation and an apostille serve different purposes. None guarantees acceptance by the German recipient.

German Translation

The recipient may require German rather than English, including a translation by a publicly sworn or recognized translator. An English version is not automatically sufficient, and a translation from a Chinese company is not automatically accepted.

The German missions explain that acceptance of a translation made abroad is decided by the individual authority. If a German sworn translator is required, use the official translator directory and confirm whether the apostille and all attachments must also be translated.

Notarization

The underlying China PCC is issued through the relevant Chinese public security authority. A Chinese notarial certificate is a separate instrument recording the specific notarial matter; its evidential scope depends on the act performed.

For the Chinese-document apostille route, the German missions’ China-specific guidance describes obtaining a notarial certificate for use abroad before the apostille. Do not assume that the original police letter can be apostilled directly. Confirm the correct notarial form with the competent office and recipient.

Apostille

The Apostille Convention has applied between mainland China and Germany since 7 November 2023. For covered public documents, an apostille replaces consular legalization; it is not an additional German embassy legalization step.

Ask whether authentication is required in your particular file. An apostille authenticates origin-related matters such as signature, signing capacity and seal; it does not certify the truth of the document’s contents, meet translation requirements by itself or guarantee acceptance.

The German missions’ translation guidance and the Federal Foreign Office’s guidance on foreign public documents explain these distinctions. A translation as an expert service is not itself automatically an apostillable public document. See China PCC format samples and notarization and apostille assistance.

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a China Police Certificate for a German visa?

It depends on the visa category and the individual request. Check the German mission’s current checklist and any additional request letter. A China PCC is not a universal requirement for all German visas, residence permits or jobs. Employers and professional authorities may have separate requirements; confirm the exact document and period they need.

Can I obtain a China PCC after leaving China?

An application may still be possible, subject to the former Chinese city’s procedure, records and authorization rules. Some routes permit an online application or a representative; others require personal attendance or additional checks. We assess the available route and assist with preparation and coordination, without guaranteeing remote processing everywhere.

What documents are required?

A local route review may involve your current and former passports, Chinese visas, residence permits where applicable, temporary residence registration, dates and addresses, school or employment records and the German recipient’s request. Authorization, application forms, photos or fingerprints may be requested locally. The final list is city-specific, not a nationwide checklist.

Can I apply without a Chinese residence permit?

A residence permit and a visitor visa are different records. The responsible Chinese authority must assess whether your status, stay and available evidence meet its procedure. Send the records you have before arranging replacements or travel. We cannot assume eligibility or promise a certificate in every case.

What if I have lost my previous passport?

Provide any copies, old passport number, visas, registration records and evidence linking the old identity to your current passport. School, employment or address records may help a local review where accepted. These are not automatically valid substitutes, and missing evidence can prevent issuance.

Does Germany require a German translation?

The German recipient decides the required language and translator credentials. German translation may be needed; English is not automatically sufficient. The German missions explain that acceptance of translations made abroad is at the authority’s discretion. If a publicly sworn or recognized translator is requested, confirm those credentials and whether the complete document, apostille and attachments must be translated.

Does my China PCC need notarization?

Confirm the required document form first. A police-issued record and a Chinese notarial certificate are different documents. The German missions’ China apostille guidance describes a notarial certificate for use abroad before obtaining an apostille, rather than directly apostilling the underlying original. This document-format guidance does not mean that every German application requires an apostille or the same notarial act.

Do I need an apostille for Germany?

For covered Chinese public documents, the Apostille Convention has applied between mainland China and Germany since 7 November 2023, replacing consular legalization. Whether your recipient requires authentication and which document should carry it must be confirmed. An apostille concerns origin-related matters, not the truth of the contents, translation quality or guaranteed acceptance.

How long does it take to obtain a China PCC?

There is no reliable nationwide fixed time. Processing depends on the Chinese city, available records, identity checks and authority review. Authorization, German translation, notarization, apostille and international delivery can add time. We give an estimate after reviewing the case, without guaranteeing completion by a particular date.

What if the Chinese authorities cannot issue my certificate?

Keep evidence of the application, attempts to obtain the record and any written response, if available. Explain the specific problem to the German recipient and ask what evidence it will consider. There is no verified universal German substitute or automatic waiver. An explanation or alternative record is acceptable only if the recipient agrees; do not fabricate a certificate or assume acceptance.
