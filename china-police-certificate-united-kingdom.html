Overview

Do You Need a China PCC for the UK?

A China police certificate may be needed for particular UK visa routes, employment screening or professional checks. The request depends on your application, occupation and residence history—not simply on having lived in China.

For specified Skilled Worker entry-clearance roles, Appendix Skilled Worker links the certificate requirement to the occupation code and qualifying overseas stays. Partner entry-clearance rules can also apply. Do not extend this rule automatically to every UK visa, in-country extension, settlement or citizenship application.

A China PCC concerns Chinese records. A DBS check does not comprehensively cover overseas criminal records and is not an automatic substitute. For China-side assistance, see our China Police Certificate Service. We are independent of the Home Office, DBS and Chinese authorities and do not provide immigration decisions.

Document Preparation

Required Documents: Start With Your China Records

This is a preparation guide, not a nationwide mandatory checklist. Requirements depend on nationality, city, residence period and the local authority’s review.

Identity & Travel History

  • Current passport bio page.
  • Former passport used during your stay in China, if available.
  • Chinese visas, entry/exit stamps and residence permits, as applicable.
  • Evidence explaining a name change or passport-number mismatch.

Residence & Authorization

  • Temporary Residence Registration Form, where applicable.
  • Dates and addresses in each Chinese city.
  • Work or school records that may help locate archives.
  • Power of attorney or other authorization, where permitted.
  • Photos or fingerprints only if the local procedure requests them.

Chinese citizens may also need identity, hukou and local residence records. A police-issued record and a notarial certificate are different documents. GOV.UK’s China directory describes a local police-to-notary route, but its China entry was last updated in August 2016. Confirm the current process with the relevant PSB and notary office; do not treat that entry’s old time estimate as a current nationwide standard.

Applying From Overseas

Tell us your former Chinese cities, residence dates, current location and available documents. We check whether authorization or a representative is permitted before you sign or send originals. Some steps may require personal attendance; fully remote processing is not available everywhere.

The GOV.UK China entry directs representatives to consult the responsible PSB and says Chinese embassies and consulates do not accept these applications. Local procedures must be confirmed. See applying after leaving China.

Translation, Notarization & Authentication

For the Skilled Worker document checklist, documents not in English or Welsh require a certified translation. GOV.UK translation guidance describes certification. For specified immigration documents, paragraph 39B(f) requires a full, independently verifiable translation with a date, accuracy confirmation, name and signature of the translator or authorized company official, and contact details. In-country leave-to-remain or indefinite-leave applications have additional translator qualification and credential requirements. Employers may use their own standards.

Notarization concerns a specific notarial act. An apostille authenticates origin-related matters such as a signature or seal; it does not establish the truth of the contents or guarantee acceptance. Neither is an automatic requirement for every UK request.

For documents covered by the Apostille Convention between China and the UK, an apostille replaces the traditional consular legalization route where authentication is required. Check scope, exemptions and the recipient’s instructions. View PCC format samples and document legalization services.

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a China Police Certificate for a UK visa?

For specified Skilled Worker entry-clearance occupations, the rules require certificates for overseas countries where you spent a total of at least 12 months in the previous 10 years while aged 18 or over. Relevant partner entry-clearance applications also have a requirement. Check the current occupation code and your own route; these conditions do not apply to every visa.

Does every UK visa require overseas police clearance?

No. Check the requirements for your visa category, application location and any later request. Do not assume that visitor, student, family, settlement, citizenship and in-country applications all follow the Skilled Worker entry-clearance checklist. Employment or professional screening can be a separate process.

Can I obtain a China PCC after leaving China?

It may be possible after departure, depending on the former Chinese city, available records and authorization requirements. We assess the local route and help prepare materials or coordinate a representative where permitted. Some procedures require personal attendance, so remote completion cannot be promised in every case.

What documents do I need to apply?

A review commonly starts with your current and previous passports, Chinese visas, residence permits where applicable, temporary residence registration and address history. Employment or study records, authorization, photos or fingerprints may be requested locally. Send the UK checklist and deadline too. The final requirements are city-specific, not a nationwide fixed list.

Can I apply without a Chinese residence permit?

A visa and a residence permit are different records. Tell us your nationality, visa type, Chinese cities and dates so that the responsible office can assess the available route. Do not assume that a local authority will issue a certificate for every visitor or accept alternative records automatically.

What if I have lost my old passport?

Provide the old passport number, any copies, previous Chinese visas, registration records and employment or study information that remain available. These can help connect historical records to your current identity, but are not guaranteed substitutes. Missing records may delay or prevent issuance; assess the case before ordering replacement documents.

Does the UK require an English translation?

For Skilled Worker applications, documents not in English or Welsh require a certified translation. Specified immigration documents must meet paragraph 39B(f), including a full translation, date, accuracy confirmation, translator or authorized company official’s name and signature, and contact details. Additional qualification and credential requirements apply to leave-to-remain applications. Confirm the recipient’s current standard.

Do I need notarization or an apostille?

Check the requested document version first. A police-issued record, a notarial certificate, a translation and an apostille serve different purposes. Do not order every step automatically. Where Convention authentication applies, an apostille replaces consular legalization; it does not prove the underlying content or guarantee UK acceptance.

How long does it take to obtain a China PCC?

There is no dependable completion time for every Chinese city and applicant. Records, identity checks, local workload, authorization and additional requests affect timing. Translation, notarization, authentication and international delivery can add time. We provide an estimate after reviewing the case, without promising a fixed completion date or outcome.

What if the Chinese authorities cannot issue my certificate?

Keep evidence of your requests and the authority’s response. Where the relevant Immigration Rules allow it, submit a reasoned explanation of why obtaining the certificate is not reasonably practicable. The Home Office assesses whether the explanation is satisfactory; this is not an automatic exemption. Employers and other recipients make their own decisions.
