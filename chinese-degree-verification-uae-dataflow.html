Chinese Degree Verification for the UAE: DataFlow, Recognition and Attestation Explained

A practical China-side guide for graduates preparing university records, identity evidence, verification, recognition and attestation for use in the United Arab Emirates.

By Global Attest Document Services Team · Published: September 6, 2026
Direct answer: A Chinese qualification used in the UAE may involve primary-source verification, qualification recognition and document attestation. They are different processes. First identify the UAE ministry, employer, licensing body or other recipient controlling your case; then obtain its current written requirements before arranging China-side documents.

If you earned a degree at a Chinese university and now need it for work, professional licensing, further study or another UAE purpose, do not start by ordering every available certificate. A DataFlow request may be part of a particular recognition or licensing route, but it is not a universal requirement for every Chinese graduate or every use in the UAE.

Your practical task is to match the correct Chinese source documents to the exact UAE process. This may require a degree certificate, graduation certificate, transcript, university response, CHSI/CSSD report, translation, consent form or attestation. Completing one step does not automatically complete the others.

Do You Need Verification, Recognition or Attestation?

Qualification recognitionDetermines whether a foreign higher-education qualification meets the responsible UAE authority’s recognition rulesUAE MoHESR or another competent authority for the relevant purposeDoes not mean every regulator or employer must accept the qualification for every purposeVerified qualification and the supporting documents specified in the current application

The distinction matters. A positive verification result can support a later decision, but it does not guarantee recognition. An attested certificate can still be subject to academic verification or recognition. A CHSI/CSSD report may provide useful Chinese academic-record evidence without automatically replacing a DataFlow process required by the UAE recipient.

How Chinese Degree Verification Fits into the UAE Process

The UAE does not have one document path for every graduate. The correct route depends on the purpose and authority. An applicant seeking a MoHESR Certificate of Recognition follows the current recognition service. A healthcare professional applying in Dubai or Abu Dhabi may instead encounter regulator-specific verification and licensing steps. An employer may request a different combination of verification and attestation.

In April 2025, the UAE Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research announced updated recognition rules for qualifications from foreign higher-education institutions. The announcement identifies authenticity and the lawful establishment, licensing and accreditation of the issuing institution as key conditions. It does not turn verification and the final recognition decision into the same act.

For the MoHESR recognition route, DataFlow’s current service information describes degree verification as the first stage before an applicant proceeds to the ministry’s recognition stage. For other uses, start with the requesting organization’s own instructions rather than assuming that the MoHESR path applies.

A useful working sequence: identify the UAE recipient → obtain its requirements → prepare Chinese source records → complete required primary-source verification → apply for recognition or licensing where required → complete attestation only if the recipient’s route also requires it.

What Does DataFlow Primary-Source Verification Check?

Primary-source verification is designed to confirm whether submitted credentials are authentic by checking with the issuing organization or another accepted source. In a Chinese degree case, the issuing university may therefore be asked to confirm the award or provide a response through its official channel.

DataFlow’s current MoHESR degree-verification guidance separates document screening, verification with the issuing organization, quality review and the later MoHESR recognition application. It also tells applicants to follow the document and translation requirements shown in the current service.

A DataFlow report is not the university certificate itself. It does not amend the university’s records, determine academic equivalency, issue a UAE professional licence or guarantee that another authority will accept the same report. Scope can also differ between regulators.

For example, the Dubai Health Authority professional-licensing information places DataFlow primary-source verification within its healthcare licensing sequence, while the Department of Health – Abu Dhabi FAQs describe a separate DoH application and verification workflow. These examples show why applicants must identify the regulator before ordering a report.

Which Chinese Academic Documents May Be Requested?

The current checklist in your application controls. Depending on the qualification and recipient, the China-side file may include:

  • Degree certificate (学位证书): evidence that an academic degree was awarded;
  • Graduation certificate (毕业证书): evidence that the student completed and graduated from the program;
  • Academic transcript (成绩单): the course-and-grade record issued or confirmed by the university;
  • University verification response: a confirmation sent by the responsible university office through the accepted channel;
  • CHSI/CSSD report: an applicable qualification, degree or transcript verification report;
  • Translation: a university-issued language version or a professional/legal translation meeting the recipient’s rules; and
  • Identity and authorization evidence: such as a current passport, historical identity details, consent form or authority-specific authorization.

These documents are not interchangeable. A degree certificate is not the same as a graduation certificate. A transcript does not prove every fact shown on the certificates. A translation does not authenticate the source record. A CHSI/CSSD report does not become a university reply merely because it concerns the same qualification.

If you are unsure which Chinese record the request names, use the China Academic Document Guides or review China degree and academic-record verification support before arranging duplicate work.

What If the Chinese University Does Not Respond?

First confirm that the request reached the current office responsible for the record. Depending on the degree level, graduation year and student category, that may be the Academic Affairs Office, Registrar, Graduate School, Degree Office, International Students Office or University Archives.

Use an official university email address or the channel stated by the university. Include enough information to route the request: the applicant’s name used during study, university, faculty, major, degree level, admission and graduation years, student number if known, certificate number if available, and the DataFlow case or reference information requested by the verifier.

The graduate may need to sign consent or authorization before the university can discuss a record with a third party. Some universities communicate only with the graduate or require their own authorization form. A representative cannot assume that permission to collect a document also authorizes disclosure to a verifier.

If the first office does not respond, redirect the enquiry to the likely record-holding office rather than repeatedly emailing an office that only supported the student during enrollment. Keep a clear record of dates, addresses and responses, and follow the verifier’s official process for supplying additional information or addressing an “unable to verify” status.

Neither an applicant nor Global Attest can compel a university to reply. Global Attest may help identify a likely office and coordinate Chinese-language communication, but the university controls its records and response.

Old Passport, Changed Name or Forgotten Student Number

Historic identity details can matter because the Chinese university or CHSI record may use the name and passport number held during study. A renewed passport does not automatically update those old records.

Prepare a simple identity-matching file containing the current passport, former passport number or scan if available, name used at the university, date of birth, previous Chinese visa or residence permit, study dates, faculty, major and copies of certificates or transcripts. If the name changed, include the official evidence accepted by the requesting organization.

A forgotten student number does not automatically make verification impossible, but it may slow an archive search. The university decides whether the remaining identifiers are sufficient. For the detailed China-side diagnosis, see CHSI old-passport and name-mismatch troubleshooting.

What If the Transcript or Certificate Is Missing?

Do not submit a recreated document or ask a translator to fill missing academic information. Contact the university to determine what official record can still be issued or confirmed.

An older transcript may require Academic Affairs or archive retrieval. The university may issue a current transcript, an archive-certified record or another institution-specific document based on the records it holds. See how to obtain an old Chinese university transcript after graduation.

If the original graduation or degree certificate was lost, the university normally deals with that through the applicable proof-document procedure rather than printing another identical original. Review replacement proof for a lost Chinese graduation or degree certificate before deciding what to send to the UAE recipient.

Availability, remote handling and representative rules vary by university. Obtaining the underlying record and completing UAE verification are separate problems.

Can CHSI or CSSD Support UAE Degree Verification?

CHSI is the platform maintained by CSSD for Chinese higher-education information and verification services. Depending on the registered record and applicable service, a graduate may be able to obtain qualification, degree or transcript reports.

The official CHSI/CSSD service overview explains the available online and formal verification functions. A report can provide authoritative supporting evidence about the corresponding Chinese record, but it is separate from a DataFlow report and from a UAE recognition decision.

Do not assume that a CHSI/CSSD report automatically substitutes for a university response or for DataFlow. Ask the UAE recipient whether it accepts the particular report, in which language and through which delivery route. The CHSI and CSSD academic verification reports guide explains the Chinese report types and boundaries in more detail.

Translation Requirements

Chinese universities may issue Chinese, English or bilingual records under their own procedures. A university-issued English version is different from an independent professional translation. A legal or certified translation may also have a recipient-specific meaning.

DataFlow’s current MoHESR guidance states that documents for that route must be in English or Arabic and gives specific legal-translation requirements when they are not. Another regulator or employer may use different language, certification or delivery rules. Follow the instructions shown in the applicable UAE application rather than assuming that one translation format works everywhere.

Translation does not change the issuing source or verify the degree. It should reproduce the source faithfully without correcting names, dates, grades or titles. For a detailed comparison, see the guide to Chinese university transcript translation.

Is Attestation a Separate Step?

Yes. UAE document attestation addresses the formal authenticity of signatures and seals in the applicable document chain. It is not primary-source academic verification and it is not qualification recognition.

The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation service states that attestation certifies the authenticity of signatures and seals on documents issued inside or outside the UAE. The route for a foreign-issued document depends on its issuing authority, document type and the service instructions in force.

An attested Chinese degree may still require verification, recognition or regulator review. Likewise, a positive verification report does not remove an attestation requirement imposed for a different purpose.

For China-issued documents used in a non-Apostille route, review the China embassy and consular legalization process. The embassy legalization case study illustrates document-chain coordination but does not determine the current UAE degree-recognition requirement.

Step-by-Step Checklist for Overseas Graduates

  1. Identify the final UAE authority or recipient. Record the ministry, licensing body, employer, university or other organization that will make the decision.
  2. Obtain its current written requirements. Use the live portal, application account or official correspondence for your exact case.
  3. Separate verification, recognition and attestation. Mark which steps are required and which organization controls each one.
  4. Identify the exact Chinese academic documents. Distinguish the degree certificate, graduation certificate, transcript and any requested university response or CHSI/CSSD report.
  5. Check identity consistency. Compare current and study-period names, passport numbers, dates and certificate details; prepare change evidence where necessary.
  6. Confirm consent and authorization. Use the form and signing method accepted by the verifier and university.
  7. Arrange university or archive records. Contact the responsible office and obtain missing documents where the institution permits.
  8. Complete the specified translation, verification or attestation. Do only the steps required for the correct authority and document.
  9. Follow the prescribed delivery route. A document may need to come from the university, an official platform or the applicant through a designated portal.
  10. Monitor the case and answer requests accurately. Keep reference numbers private and respond through the official account or support channel.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does every Chinese degree need DataFlow for the UAE?

No. The requirement depends on the UAE purpose and authority. MoHESR recognition and some professional-licensing routes use primary-source verification, but applicants should follow the instructions for their specific ministry, regulator, employer or recipient.

2. Is DataFlow verification the same as UAE degree recognition?

No. DataFlow primary-source verification checks the credential against an issuing or accepted source. Recognition is the later decision made by the competent UAE authority under its own criteria.

3. Does CHSI replace DataFlow?

Not automatically. CHSI/CSSD reports may support a case, but the UAE recipient decides whether a particular report is accepted and whether its own DataFlow or university-response route remains required.

4. Does an attested degree still need verification?

It may. Attestation authenticates specified signatures and seals; it does not establish academic equivalency or replace a primary-source verification requirement.

5. What if my Chinese university does not reply?

Confirm the request reached the current record-holding office, provide accurate case and identity details, and use the verifier’s official follow-up route. A response cannot be guaranteed or compelled.

6. Is an academic transcript required?

It depends on the current application checklist. Some recognition or regulator routes request a full transcript, while another recipient may ask for a different document set.

7. What if my old passport has expired?

Prepare the current passport and available evidence linking it to the identity used during study, such as the old passport number, visa, residence permit or certificate copy. The university and UAE recipient decide whether the evidence is sufficient.

8. What if my name is different on the degree?

Do not alter the certificate or translation. Provide the study-period name, current identity and the official name-change or identity-linking evidence required by the relevant organization.

9. Can I start the process outside China?

Often the initial UAE application and China-side document review can begin remotely, but the university, verifier or attestation route may require applicant participation, specific authorization or physical documents.

10. Does positive verification guarantee recognition?

No. Verification can support the application, but MoHESR or the relevant licensing authority makes the recognition or licensing decision under its current rules.

11. Can Global Attest contact my Chinese university?

Global Attest may help identify and communicate with the responsible office where permitted. It cannot impersonate the graduate, bypass consent requirements or compel the university to respond.

12. How long does the process take?

There is no reliable universal timeframe. It depends on the authority, document scope, university responsiveness, identity matching, translation and any additional checks. Use the current estimate shown in your official application and do not treat it as a guarantee.
