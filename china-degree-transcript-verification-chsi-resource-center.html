What Are CHSI and CSSD?

Understand CHSI degree verification, CHSI transcript verification, CSSD reports and the role of Chinese academic verification in international education and credential evaluation.

What Is CHSI?

CHSI is commonly used to refer to China’s higher education student information and academic credential verification platform. The official CHSI website maintained by CSSD describes services involving student records, qualification certificates, degree certificates and academic transcripts.

Independent resource: This is a Global Attest information page, not the CHSI or CSSD official website. To sign in, apply for a report or check a report, use the current official platform. CHSI/CSSD determines account access, available report services, verification functions and technical outcomes. Start from the official CHSI/CSSD English portal or the official report-checking page.

For broader help with degree certificates, transcripts, university records, WES and overseas use, use the China Academic Document Guides hub. Use this page to compare CHSI/CSSD report types and identify the relevant official route.

CHSI verification reports

A CHSI verification report presents verified academic information in a format that can be checked through the issuing platform. Available report types and application requirements depend on the academic record and the current platform options.

For a complete overview of Chinese academic document certification, read our China Degree Certification Guide.

CHSI and CSSD services and instructions may change. Applicants should use the official platform pages linked above for current report and account requirements.

What Is CSSD?

CSSD is the English name used by the Center for Student Services and Development. Its official verification-service overview describes services concerning higher-education qualification certificates, student records and college transcripts. In international instructions, CHSI and CSSD may appear in connection with academic verification reports, platform services or document submission routes. Applicants should follow the terminology and report type stated by the receiving authority.

How to Verify a CHSI / CSSD Report

Identify the required report, confirm the official channel, obtain and review the report, then follow the receiving institution’s submission requirements.

Application: requesting an official report through the applicable platform route.

Verification: checking an issued report using its applicable official verification service.

Optional assistance: Global Attest can help review available records and requirements; it does not issue official verification reports.

  1. Identify whether the receiving authority needs a degree verification report, graduation certificate verification report, transcript verification report, original academic record, translation or a combination of these documents.
  2. Use the current official platform route for the relevant academic record and provide the information or supporting material requested for that report type. See CSSD's current verification-report application guidance.
  3. Review the issued report language, report number, verification report validity, QR code and online verification information before submission.
  4. Confirm whether the receiving organization requires direct delivery, an online verification link, a downloadable report or applicant upload. CSSD publishes separate digital-transmission guidance for supported recipient routes.

A report number, online verification code or other function may allow the receiving party to check a report, depending on the report format and current platform service. Use the official CSSD report-checking page for the report categories it supports.

Degree, Graduation Certificate and Transcript Verification

Degree verification, graduation certificate verification and academic transcript verification address different records. A degree report concerns the academic degree awarded, a graduation report concerns completion of the academic program, and a transcript report concerns the detailed study record. A receiving organization may request one or several of these reports.

If the original graduation or degree certificate has been lost, first review how to request replacement proof from the Chinese university before determining the applicable CHSI/CSSD verification route.

If you cannot contact the Chinese university, a transcript or qualification record is missing, historical passport, name or student-number details do not match, the recipient requests additional university confirmation, or China-side coordination is needed, review our China degree and transcript verification support. Global Attest cannot control a CHSI/CSSD account, alter an official record or guarantee a university, platform or recipient outcome.

Common Document Checks

Submitting only the degree certificate

A recipient may request a specified CHSI or CSSD verification report rather than relying only on a scanned diploma.

Wrong report language

Some agencies require English reports, while others may accept Chinese reports with a translation that meets their requirements.

Expired or unverifiable report

The official CHSI online-verification service overview describes validity and extension for certain online verification reports. Other report types may be handled differently. Use the CHSI verification report extension guide to identify what can be extended and when a different route may be needed.

Mixing transcript and degree requirements

Degree verification and transcript verification are different documents, and the recipient decides whether one or both are required.

WES may require verified Chinese academic records as part of its credential evaluation process. Frequently Asked Questions

What is CHSI?

CHSI is commonly used to describe China’s higher education student information and academic credential verification platform. It provides access to certain education records and online verification reports for Chinese degrees, graduation credentials and academic transcripts. Universities, credential evaluators, employers and other overseas authorities may use these reports to check submitted academic information. Available services and report formats depend on the record type and current official platform options.

What is the difference between CHSI and CSSD?

CHSI is the widely recognized name associated with China’s higher education information website and verification platform. CSSD is the English name used for the student services and development center connected with academic verification services. International agencies may refer to either term in their instructions. Applicants should follow the exact report type and submission method requested rather than assuming that every CHSI or CSSD document serves the same purpose.

Which Chinese academic documents can be verified?

Depending on the available official records and platform services, verification may cover Chinese degree certificates, graduation certificates and academic transcripts. Degree, graduation and transcript reports confirm different parts of an academic history and are not automatically interchangeable. Some reports may be available in Chinese or English and may include online verification details. Applicants should check which document, language and report format the receiving institution requires before submitting an application.

Is CHSI verification required for WES?

CHSI or CSSD verification is often relevant for Chinese academic credentials submitted to WES, but requirements can vary by credential, education history and the instructions attached to an individual application. Applicants should use the current document checklist in their WES account and follow the specified delivery method. A CHSI verification report confirms academic information; it does not determine the credential equivalency or guarantee the final evaluation issued by WES.

How long is a CHSI verification report valid?

Validity depends on the report type, issue settings and current official platform rules. Some online verification reports display a validity period and may offer an official way to extend or update access, while other reports may be handled differently. Check the date, report number, QR code and online verification status before submission. The receiving institution may also impose its own recency requirements, so applicants should confirm both platform and recipient instructions.
