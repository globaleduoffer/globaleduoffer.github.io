Common questions about our services

Can I apply for a China Police Certificate remotely?

In many cases, former residents of China can start the China PCC process remotely. Requirements depend on the city, residence history and destination authority.

Can you help with WES credential evaluation?

Yes. Global Attest can help review academic document requirements, coordinate Chinese university materials and support WES-related document preparation.

Do you handle apostille and consular legalization?

Yes. Global Attest supports Hague apostille, notarization and embassy or consular legalization for China-related documents depending on destination country requirements.

How soon will Global Attest reply?

Global Attest normally reviews new inquiries within one business day. WhatsApp is usually the fastest way to reach the team.
