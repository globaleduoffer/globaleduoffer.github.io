First Identify Who Controls the Next Step

A document problem is easier to solve once you identify the body responsible for the blocked stage. The university generally controls its source records, transcript issuance and institution-specific archive procedures. CHSI/CSSD controls its account, verification and report functions within the services it provides. WES controls the Required Documents instructions, accepted delivery channels, application status and evaluation decision for a WES file. A notarial office, authentication authority or overseas recipient controls a separate notarization, Apostille or acceptance requirement.

Start with the exact message or request you received. Record the document name, purpose, submission deadline and required sender. Then compare that request with the documents you hold and any online record or report available to you. This prevents a retrieval problem from being treated as a verification problem, or a WES delivery issue from being treated as a university-record error.

Your location can affect coordination but does not change who owns the official decision. A former student outside China may be able to begin document comparison, university contact and platform troubleshooting remotely. However, a university or official system may still require the graduate’s participation, identity evidence, an accepted authorization, original documents or another institution-specific step.

Know Which Chinese Academic Document You Need

The recipient may be asking for a physical certificate, a university record, an official verification report or an overseas authentication step. These are not interchangeable.

学籍 — student status / student record

Information about enrollment and study status. It is not the same as graduation, qualification or an academic degree.

毕业证书 — graduation certificate

Evidence that the student completed and graduated from the relevant program. It is distinct from an academic degree certificate.

学位证书 — degree certificate

Evidence that the relevant academic degree was awarded. A graduate can hold both a graduation certificate and a separate degree certificate.

学历 — educational qualification / higher-education qualification

The formally registered education qualification associated with completion of a recognized program. It is distinct from the academic degree itself.

学位 — academic degree

The academic degree awarded, such as a bachelor’s, master’s or doctoral degree. It is distinct from the graduation or qualification record.

成绩单 — academic transcript

A course-and-grade record issued or confirmed through the university. Its issuance, verification and delivery route can be separate questions.

CHSI/CSSD verification report

An official report for an available qualification, degree or transcript verification service. The correct report depends on the record and recipient instructions.

University-issued replacement, certification or archive document

Institution-specific evidence that may be available when an original certificate is lost or historical records must be confirmed.

If the distinction is unclear, see the difference between a Chinese degree certificate and graduation certificate.

Retrieval, Verification, WES and Overseas Use Are Different Routes

Retrieve

Obtain an available certificate, transcript, certification or archive evidence from the responsible institution.

Verify

Confirm an existing qualification, degree or transcript through the applicable official academic-record route.

Submit

Follow WES or another recipient’s current requirements for document source, format and delivery.

Authenticate

Use notarization, Apostille or legalization only when the document and receiving authority require that separate process.

Translation can support one of these routes, but it does not replace the required source academic document.

Lost Certificates and Historical University Records

If an original certificate has been lost, the available response may involve a university-issued certification, archive-based evidence or another institution-specific document rather than an identical replacement original. The available form must be confirmed with the responsible university.

Older graduates may need an archive search, university confirmation or help locating the successor institution if a school has merged or changed its name. Procedures, authorization rules and available records vary by institution. Foreign graduates who are outside China can review document services for international students and former graduates, but applicant participation or university-specific authorization may still be required.

CHSI/CSSD Verification Is Not a WES Evaluation

CHSI is the public platform associated with Chinese higher-education information services, while CSSD performs designated verification functions and issues relevant reports. Qualification, degree and transcript reports have different purposes. Eligibility and availability depend on the underlying record and current official service. Check the official CHSI/CSSD platform for its current services and instructions.

WES is a separate credential-evaluation organization. It decides which documents, reports and delivery routes apply to an individual application and produces its own evaluation. A CSSD report can support an applicable WES route, but it does not itself decide equivalency or guarantee acceptance. The applicant’s current WES Required Documents instructions control the applicable WES route.

Using Chinese Academic Documents Overseas

For a degree document intended for formal overseas use, start with the current China degree Apostille requirements. The document route may depend on whether the recipient needs an original, a notarial certificate, a certified translation or an Apostille. Consult the current China MFA Apostille information alongside the receiving organization’s instructions.

A degree and a graduation certificate may follow different evidence requirements. Academic transcripts also have their own documentary issues; use the China academic transcript Apostille guide when a recipient requests authentication of a transcript.

Keep the functions separate: notarization records a notarial act, an Apostille authenticates the origin of a qualifying public document, and neither process creates an academic equivalency decision or replaces CHSI/CSSD or WES requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Chinese academic document do I need?

That depends on what the receiving organization is trying to confirm. It may require a graduation certificate, degree certificate, transcript, CHSI/CSSD verification report, translation or authenticated document. Start with the recipient’s current instructions and match the named document to the routes above.

What is the difference between a Chinese degree certificate and graduation certificate?

A graduation certificate records completion and graduation from a program. A degree certificate records the award of an academic degree. They are distinct documents, and a recipient may request one or both.

Is CHSI/CSSD verification the same as a WES evaluation?

No. CHSI/CSSD verification confirms available Chinese academic information through its applicable report services. WES independently determines its required documents and evaluates credentials for its own report.

What should I do if my Chinese university record cannot be found?

First identify whether the missing item is a qualification record, degree record, transcript report or account connection. A missing online record does not by itself prove the credential is invalid; use the dedicated missing-record guide to diagnose the next contact.

Can I obtain old Chinese university documents after leaving China?

Possibly, but availability and procedure are institution-specific. The university may require archive information, identity evidence, applicant participation or an authorization it accepts. Retrieval and the form of any replacement or certification cannot be guaranteed.

Do Chinese academic documents need notarization or Apostille?

Only when the receiving organization and intended use require that route. Notarization or Apostille is separate from academic verification and WES evaluation, and it does not guarantee overseas acceptance.
