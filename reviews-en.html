FAQ

Are these comments from real clients?

The comments in the Direct Client Feedback section were provided directly by clients. Personal names, exact dates and sensitive case information are not displayed.

Why are client names not displayed?

Descriptive identity labels identify the service context, not a client’s real name. We use these labels to protect client privacy. Exact dates are omitted when they are unavailable or cannot be reliably confirmed.

Are these the same as Trustpilot reviews?

No. Direct client feedback on this page is separate from reviews published independently on Trustpilot.

What is the difference between client feedback and case summaries?

Direct client feedback contains comments provided by clients. Case summaries are written from Global Attest service records and are not presented as verbatim customer reviews.

Can clients request removal of their feedback?

Yes. Please contact Global Attest at chinaattest@gmail.com to request removal of your feedback. Do not include passport numbers or sensitive documents in a public review.

How can I leave a review?

You can send feedback through our contact page or email chinaattest@gmail.com. You can also visit our public Trustpilot profile to leave an independent review. Please leave out sensitive personal or document information.
