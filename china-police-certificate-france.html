Overview

Do You Need a China PCC for France?

A French authority, employer or institution may request evidence of your criminal-record history in China. Whether a China Police Certificate is needed depends on the procedure, your circumstances and the actual recipient’s checklist. It is not a universal requirement for every French visa, residence or employment application.

For a visa, start with the official France-Visas assistant and your personalized supporting-document list. Other procedures have their own rules: for example, the official naturalisation application guidance describes foreign criminal-record evidence in specified circumstances. Those conditions must not be applied to all visa or residence applications.

Issuance in China and acceptance in France are separate decisions. Confirm the requested period, issue date, police or notarial document form, translation language and authentication requirements before ordering additional services. A French criminal-record extract is a different document from a China PCC.

Document Preparation

Required Documents: Start With Your China Records

This is a preparation guide, not a nationwide mandatory checklist. Requirements depend on nationality, city, residence period and the local authority’s review.

Identity & Travel History

  • Current passport bio page.
  • Former passport used during your stay in China, if available.
  • Chinese visas, entry/exit stamps and residence permits, as applicable.
  • Evidence explaining a name change or passport-number mismatch.

Residence & Authorization

  • Temporary Residence Registration Form, where applicable.
  • Dates and addresses in each Chinese city.
  • Work or school records that may help locate archives.
  • Power of attorney or other authorization, where permitted.
  • Photos or fingerprints only if the local procedure requests them.

This is not a nationwide mandatory list. Chinese citizens may also need identity, hukou and local records; foreign applicants follow the relevant local procedure. Bring the French request letter and any appointment or submission deadline to the route review. Additional forms, photographs or fingerprints are needed only where the local authority requests them. We cannot guarantee record recovery or certificate issuance.

Applying From Outside China

How to Get a China PCC for France After Leaving China

Confirm the Responsible City and Route

Leaving China does not automatically rule out an application. Send your former Chinese cities, residence dates, current country, passport history and available records. An online route or authorized representative may be possible only where the responsible local authority permits it.

Old passports, visas, residence permits and temporary residence registration can affect eligibility and record checks. A visitor visa and a residence permit are different records. Some routes require personal attendance, additional identity checks or a locally specified Power of Attorney.

Read the guide to applying after leaving China.

Plan Around Missing Records and Deadlines

For lost passports, missing registration, several Chinese cities or inconsistent names, first check which evidence can be verified locally. Archived or reissued records may be explored where the procedure allows; availability is not assured. School or employment records are not automatically accepted replacements.

Send the French recipient’s exact checklist and appointment date before preparing authorization, translation or delivery. If the certificate cannot be issued, ask that recipient what explanation or alternative evidence it will consider. There is no verified universal French substitute or automatic waiver.

France Document Format

Does a China Police Certificate for France Need French Translation or an Apostille?

Confirm the required document chain first. A police-issued record, a Chinese notarial certificate, a French translation and an apostille have different functions. None by itself guarantees acceptance by the French recipient.

French Translation

The recipient may require French and a particular translator qualification or certification method. English is not automatically a substitute. A French translation supplied by a Chinese company is not automatically accepted.

If traduction assermentée is requested, verify the accepted translator and whether the apostille and attachments must also be translated. Service-Public’s translator guidance explains approved judicial experts in France and the consular-list route abroad; additional formalities can depend on the translation’s origin and use.

Notarization

The underlying China PCC is issued by the responsible Chinese public security authority. A Chinese notarial certificate is a separate document recording a specified notarial act; it must not be treated as the same instrument.

Ask the French recipient which form it needs and the competent Chinese office which instrument it can process. Do not assume that every French application requires notarization, or that every police-issued record must first follow the same notarial route.

Apostille

The Apostille Convention has applied between mainland China and France since 7 November 2023. France’s official document-classification table identifies an apostille route for Chinese notarial acts and administrative records, subject to its category notes and exceptions.

For covered documents, apostille replaces consular legalization; they are not interchangeable names or two routine consecutive steps. Check the actual document, any exemption and recipient requirements. An apostille authenticates origin-related matters, not the contents, translation quality or guaranteed acceptance.

The Convention excludes certain instruments, including documents executed by diplomatic or consular agents and administrative documents dealing directly with commercial or customs operations. The French table treats those categories separately. Do not apply a blanket rule to every Chinese document. See the French guidance on foreign public documents, China PCC format samples and notarization and apostille assistance.

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a China Police Certificate for a French visa?

It depends on the visa category, your circumstances and the French authority’s request. Use the France-Visas assistant and your personalized document checklist. A China PCC is not a universal requirement for every French visa or residence application. Other administrative procedures and employers can have separate requirements; confirm the document form, period and issue date they need.

Can I obtain a China PCC after leaving China?

An application may still be possible, depending on the former Chinese city’s procedure, records and authorization rules. Some routes permit an online application or a representative; others require personal attendance or additional checks. We assess the available route and assist with preparation and coordination, without guaranteeing remote processing everywhere.

What documents are required?

A local route review may involve your current and former passports, Chinese visas, residence permits where applicable, temporary residence registration, dates and addresses, school or employment records and the French recipient’s request. Authorization, forms, photographs or fingerprints may be requested locally. The final list is city-specific, not a nationwide checklist.

Can I apply without a Chinese residence permit?

A residence permit and a visitor visa are different records. The responsible Chinese authority must assess whether your status, stay and available evidence meet its procedure. Send the records you have before arranging replacements or travel. We cannot assume eligibility or promise a certificate in every case.

What if I have lost my previous passport?

Provide any copies, old passport number, visas, registration records and evidence linking your old identity to the current passport. School, employment or address records may assist a local review where accepted. They are not automatically valid substitutes, and missing evidence can prevent issuance.

Does France require a French translation?

The French recipient decides the language, translator qualification and certification method it accepts. If a sworn translation is required, verify the accepted translator and whether the apostille and attachments also need translation. English is not automatically sufficient, and a translation from a Chinese company is not automatically accepted. Official French guidance distinguishes approved judicial experts in France and translation arrangements abroad.

Does my China PCC need notarization?

Confirm the required document form first. A police-issued China PCC and a Chinese notarial certificate are different instruments. A French recipient may request a particular notarial form, but notarization is not a universal rule for all French applications. The competent Chinese office must also confirm which document it can process for any requested apostille.

Do I need an apostille for France?

The Apostille Convention has applied between mainland China and France since 7 November 2023. For covered public documents, an apostille replaces consular legalization. France’s official table provides an apostille route for Chinese notarial acts and administrative records, subject to category notes and exceptions. Confirm whether authentication is needed for your particular file and which instrument should carry it. An apostille does not certify contents or guarantee acceptance.

How long does it take to obtain a China PCC?

There is no reliable nationwide fixed time. Processing depends on the Chinese city, available records, identity checks and authority review. Authorization, French translation, notarization, apostille and international delivery can add time. We give an estimate after reviewing the case, without guaranteeing completion by a particular date.

What if the Chinese authorities cannot issue my certificate?

Keep evidence of the application, attempts to obtain the record and any written response, if available. Explain the specific obstacle to the French recipient and ask what evidence its procedure allows. There is no verified universal French substitute or automatic waiver. An explanation or alternative record is acceptable only if the recipient agrees; do not fabricate a certificate or assume acceptance.
