China Apostille for Commercial and Company Documents: What Form Should You Prepare?

Identify the corporate fact, record source and document form before assessing an Apostille route for overseas use.

By Global Attest Document Services Team · Published: August 27, 2026
Direct answer: A Chinese company document may be capable of entering an Apostille route, but the correct preparation depends on what the overseas recipient needs and whether the available item is an official record, certified extract, notarial certificate or private corporate document. A Business Licence, resolution, Power of Attorney, contract and Articles of Association should not be treated as interchangeable or assumed to follow the same route.

This article focuses on document-form identification before processing. For the general Convention route and application stages, review the China Apostille requirements and process.

Which Chinese Company Document Does the Overseas Recipient Actually Need?

Start with the corporate fact the recipient wants proved: registration, company particulars, signing authority, transaction approval or representative authority. Obtain its written instructions and identify the accepted record. A Business Licence, resolution and Power of Attorney serve different purposes. Begin preparation only after the required fact and form are clear.

Company Documents Do Not All Have the Same Apostille Status

A document title alone does not determine Apostille eligibility. The Hague Apostille Convention covers public documents including notarial acts and official certificates on private documents. The HCCH Apostille Handbook explains that public status is determined under the law of the place of origin; a private company record does not become public merely because it concerns business.

The Convention also excludes certain administrative documents dealing directly with commercial or customs operations. The HCCH Explanatory Report treats that exclusion narrowly; it should not be read as excluding all commercial or company documents.

Common Chinese Company Documents and Their Possible Forms

Business Licence

A Chinese Business Licence may be capable of entering an Apostille route in an eligible official or appropriately prepared form. That could involve the original, an official registry record, a certified or notarized copy, or a notarial certificate. The authority and overseas recipient should confirm the form.

SAMR guidance gives electronic and paper Business Licences equivalent domestic legal effect. It does not establish identical Apostille handling. A screenshot, printout or photocopy is not automatically an official original.

Company Registry and Registration Records

Public company information is not necessarily an official registry extract. Forms include official or certified records, online information, screenshots, printouts and notarized evidence. Identify the issuer, certification and date, then confirm what the recipient accepts.

Articles of Association

“Articles of Association” describes content, not one document status. The item may be company-held, filed, authority-issued, officially certified or notarized. Articles are not automatically public and do not always require notarization.

Board and Shareholder Resolutions

A resolution is generally a private corporate act. A competent body may first certify a signature, seal, copy or fact, after which an Apostille may authenticate the resulting public act. A company chop or representative's signature does not automatically make it public.

Company Power of Attorney

A corporate POA review may involve company identity, signer, authority, signature or chop and powers granted. A notarial or official act may be needed. An Apostille does not prove the signer possessed the claimed corporate authority. Review the China Power of Attorney Apostille guide for the POA-specific form and notarization-first decision.

Commercial Contracts and Authorization Letters

A commercial contract is generally private. An official act concerning its copy, signature, seal or date may be needed before Apostille. Signing or stamping a contract does not make it directly Apostillable, and Apostille does not validate it.

When Might Notarization or an Official Certification Be Needed First?

The PRC Notarization Law allows notaries to handle matters including contracts, entrustments, Articles of Association, signatures, seals, dates and copy-original conformity. That means notarization may be available for relevant company-document issues; it does not mean notarization is required before every Apostille. Review the difference between Apostille and notarization before selecting a sequence.

An Apostille authenticates formal origin elements identified in the certificate, such as the signature, capacity or seal of the public official or notary. It does not independently establish the validity of a private company chop, the signer's corporate authority or the legality of the transaction.

What an Apostille Proves—and Does Not Prove—About a Company Document

China's official Apostille guidance explains that an Apostille authenticates specified formal origin elements rather than the underlying content. It does not establish good standing, representative or shareholder authority, resolution or contract validity, or the truth of every statement. Those issues remain with the issuer, applicable law and recipient.

Does the Company Document Need Translation?

No universal translation rule applies. The destination, recipient, filing language and document form may determine whether a certified translation is accepted or must form part of a notarial package. Translation is not automatically required before or after Apostille, and not every translation requires notarization.

Why the Overseas Receiving Organization Must Be Checked First

The recipient may specify the fact, document, version, recency, translation and signatory evidence. An Apostille does not select the correct corporate record. It authenticates formal elements of the qualifying public document. If a completed document is rejected, see what to check if an Apostilled Chinese document is not accepted.

Commercial Document Apostille Review Checklist

Review checklist—not universal Apostille requirements:

  • Corporate fact the recipient wants proved.
  • Document type and issuing or creating body.
  • Current form: original, copy, electronic, registry or notarial.
  • Whether it is an official record or private company act.
  • Destination and overseas receiving organization.
  • Recipient's written form, recency and translation instructions.
  • Missing certification questions and deadline.

Common Mistakes

  • Assuming every company document follows the same route.
  • Assuming every Business Licence can be Apostilled directly.
  • Treating online registry information as an official extract.
  • Assuming a company chop makes a private document public.
  • Applying one notarization sequence to every resolution or POA.
  • Treating an Apostilled copy as the original.
  • Assuming Apostille proves corporate authority or guarantees acceptance.
  • Starting before the recipient identifies the required record and translation form.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Chinese Business Licence be Apostilled?

Potentially, but the appropriate form—such as an eligible original, official registry record, certified or notarized copy, or notarial certificate—must be confirmed for the case.

Can a board or shareholder resolution be Apostilled?

A private resolution may need a qualifying official act. The sequence depends on the authority and recipient.

Can Chinese Articles of Association receive an Apostille?

Potentially, but their source and form matter. A company-held copy, filed version, certified record and notarial certificate are not interchangeable.

Does a company Power of Attorney need notarization first?

Not universally. The signer, authority evidence, form and recipient determine whether an official act is needed.

Can a commercial contract be Apostilled?

A private contract is not automatically eligible. An official act concerning the contract, copy, signature, seal or date may be required.

Do Chinese company documents need translation?

Requirements vary by destination, recipient, filing language and document form. No universal before-or-after rule applies.

Do I need an original, certified copy or notarial certificate?

Confirm the requested corporate fact and acceptable form with the recipient, then verify route eligibility with the competent authority.

Does an Apostille prove the company is valid or that a resolution is legally effective?

No. Apostille authenticates specified formal origin elements; it does not decide substantive corporate status, authority or validity.

Does an Apostille guarantee overseas acceptance?

No. The overseas receiving organization continues to control its substantive document and filing requirements.
