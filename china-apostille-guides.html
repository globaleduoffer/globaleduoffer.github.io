How to use this resource center

China Apostille requirements can vary by document type, issuing authority, destination and receiving organization. Use this hub to find the Global Attest guide that matches your question without searching through unrelated services or resources. Start with the complete guide for a general overview, or go directly to guidance on timing, notarization, overseas handling, personal documents, academic records or police certificates. Each page addresses a distinct part of the Apostille decision journey. When Apostille is not the applicable route, use the legalization section below. For case-specific assistance, the final section links to our service page for a document and route review.

Choose the page that best matches your immediate question. A document-specific guide is the better starting point when the required document form is unclear, while the timing and overseas guides focus on planning and coordination. The cards below summarize each page's scope so you can move directly to the most relevant guidance.
