OUR STORY

Connecting People, Opportunities, and Possibilities Across Borders

China has shaped the lives of international students, professionals, entrepreneurs, and families. Some once studied, worked, or lived here; others see new opportunities for education, business, and personal development.

Administrative procedures can be difficult for people unfamiliar with local requirements or now living overseas and coordinating their applications remotely.

Former residents may need a China Police Certificate for immigration or employment. Graduates may need transcripts or academic verification years after university. Others require notarization, apostille, or legalization for documents used abroad.

Foreign entrepreneurs face company registration and documentation requirements. Prospective students need guidance on university selection, admissions, Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) and university scholarship applications, and X1/X2 student visas.

These goals share a practical challenge: identifying the right procedures, preparing appropriate documents, and finding reliable support in China.

Why Global Attest Exists

Global Attest is built around a simple idea: services and opportunities connected to China should be clearer and more manageable, wherever someone lives.

Based in Shenzhen, we support international clients with China document services, company formation and business support, and university admission and scholarship assistance.

Our document work covers Police Certificates, notarization, Apostille, consular legalization, academic verification, and transcripts. We also help entrepreneurs prepare registration documents and guide students through university, scholarship, and visa requirements. We explain the relevant steps and support preparation without promising institutional decisions.

Understanding Every Client's Journey

Some applicants have complete records and need a clear process. Others face missing old passports, incomplete accommodation registration, difficulty contacting former universities, or transcripts needed years after graduation.

An entrepreneur entering China has different priorities from a graduate applying for residence overseas or a student choosing a university.

We begin with the client's actual circumstances, rather than using one approach for every case. Careful materials review, clear communication, and practical coordination guide our work.

More Than Documents and Applications

Every application supports a personal or professional goal. A Police Certificate may help with immigration; a transcript may enable further study or employment. Company registration may start a business venture, while admission may begin a student's future in China.

These procedures connect to important decisions and milestones. We aim to make them more accessible through careful preparation and dependable communication, helping clients understand their options and the steps involved.

Whether you need records from your time in China, help establishing a business, or university application support, our team helps you prepare and move forward with greater confidence.

From your past connections with China to your future opportunities here, Global Attest is here to support your journey.
