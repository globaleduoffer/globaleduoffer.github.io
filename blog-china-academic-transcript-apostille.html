China Academic Transcript Apostille: Original, Notarized or Verified?

Identify the transcript form and authentication route required for overseas use before arranging notarization or Apostille.

By Global Attest Document Services Team · Published: August 28, 2026
Direct answer: A Chinese academic transcript does not automatically need an Apostille. The overseas receiving organization decides whether Apostille is required. If it is, current China MFA guidance requires a transcript issued by a Chinese educational institution to undergo notarization in China before the eligible foreign-related notarial certificate proceeds to Apostille.

A university transcript, sealed envelope, scan or CHSI/CSSD report is not automatically the document that receives the Apostille. WES and direct-delivery requirements are separate. For broader academic-document assistance, see our China Degree Apostille services.

Does a Chinese Academic Transcript Need an Apostille?

Not automatically. An overseas university, immigration authority, licensing body, employer or credential evaluator decides what evidence it needs. Some recipients request an Apostille; others require academic verification, credential evaluation or direct delivery instead.

Apostilling a transcript that the recipient did not request can add cost and delay without satisfying the actual submission requirement. Confirm the destination, intended use and recipient instructions first.

What Counts as an Academic Transcript?

成绩单 is best described in English as an Academic Transcript. Depending on the issuing institution and recipient, related labels may include Transcript, Official Transcript, Academic Record or Grade Report. These labels are not automatically interchangeable.

Use “Official Transcript” only when the university issued or recognizes that form as official, or when the recipient defines it that way. A student-portal printout, scan or personal copy may contain useful academic information without having the same status as a university-issued record.

Why Prior Notarization Is Required for Chinese University Transcripts

Current China MFA guidance states that an academic transcript issued by a Chinese educational institution must first undergo notarization in China. After a qualifying foreign-related notarial certificate is issued, that eligible notarial document may proceed to Apostille where the destination and receiving organization require the Apostille route.

Working framework: University transcript → Chinese notarization review → qualifying foreign-related notarial certificate → Apostille, if required.

This does not mean every case uses the same notarial certificate. The precise notarial subject, supporting evidence, document form and translation arrangement may differ. Review the broader China Apostille requirements and process.

What Transcript Form Enters the Apostille Route?

University-Issued Original

A university-issued original may serve as the source record or supporting evidence reviewed during notarization. Under the MFA-listed route, avoid assuming that the original transcript itself normally goes directly to the Apostille authority. Confirm what the notary requires and what the overseas recipient expects to receive.

Copy, Scan or System Printout

A photocopy reproduces another document; it does not independently establish official status. A scan can assist preliminary review but is not automatically acceptable for notarization or Apostille. A self-printed online record does not become an official transcript merely because it came from a university system.

The acceptable evidence and resulting notarial form must be confirmed for the specific case.

Foreign-Related Notarial Certificate

After notarization, a qualifying foreign-related notarial certificate may become the public document that proceeds to the Apostille stage. Its precise form depends on the notarial matter and the requirements of the relevant authorities and receiving organization.

Notarization and Apostille perform different functions. Review the difference between Apostille and notarization before treating them as interchangeable steps.

Original, Bilingual, Multi-page or Sealed Transcript?

Bilingual and Translated Forms

A bilingual transcript does not eliminate the notarization or Apostille question. It also does not automatically satisfy the recipient's language rules. Translation requirements may depend on the transcript language, notarial certificate, destination, filing rules and receiving organization.

There is no universal rule that every transcript must be translated, every translation must be notarized, or translation must always occur at one particular stage.

Multiple-Page Transcripts

Keep every page complete and associated with the record. Do not detach, reorder or omit pages. The university, notary, competent authority and overseas receiving organization may have case-specific requirements for seals, attachment, binding or document completeness.

Do not assume that every page must be stamped, every transcript needs a riding seal, or one national binding rule applies to every case.

Sealed-Envelope Transcripts

A sealed envelope may matter to WES, a university or another recipient, but it is not itself the document that receives an Apostille. Confirm the notary's handling requirements before opening or submitting a sealed transcript.

A recipient's sealed-delivery rule and China's notarization and Apostille route are separate controls.

CHSI/CSSD Transcript Verification vs Apostille

A university Academic Transcript, a CHSI/CSSD Transcript Verification Report, a foreign-related notarial certificate and an Apostille are different records or processes.

CHSI/CSSD transcript verification may provide a separate verification record for academic information, depending on the available service and the recipient's requirements. That verification is not the university transcript, not the notarial certificate and not the Apostille. Conversely, an Apostille does not perform CSSD's academic-verification function.

Do not assume every transcript Apostille case requires CHSI/CSSD. Confirm whether the notary, Apostille authority or overseas recipient requests it. See CHSI and CSSD transcript verification for the separate verification function.

Transcript Apostille vs WES or Direct University Submission

An Apostille authenticates formal origin elements of a qualifying public or notarial document. WES controls its own credential-evaluation document and delivery rules. Direct university submission is another recipient-controlled delivery or authenticity mechanism.

None automatically replaces another. A recipient may request Apostille, direct delivery, CHSI/CSSD verification, credential evaluation, a combination, or none of them. An Apostille does not automatically satisfy WES document-verification or submission requirements. Review China transcript verification for WES separately.

What Does the Apostille Actually Prove?

Under the HCCH Apostille Convention, an Apostille certifies the authenticity of the signature, the capacity in which the signer acted and, where applicable, the identity of the seal or stamp on the qualifying public document.

It does not verify individual grades, GPA, course content or course translations. It does not establish course, credit or degree equivalency; replace CHSI/CSSD, WES or credential evaluation; or guarantee acceptance by an overseas recipient.

Check the Overseas Receiving Organization First

Before preparing the transcript, ask the recipient to confirm:

  • whether a transcript is required and which academic period it must cover;
  • whether it wants an original, copy, notarial certificate or another form;
  • whether Apostille is required;
  • whether direct university delivery or a sealed envelope is required;
  • the required language and translation certification;
  • whether CHSI/CSSD verification or credential evaluation is required; and
  • whether a Graduation Certificate or Degree Certificate is also needed.

Those certificates serve different academic purposes from a transcript. See our guide to the Graduation Certificate and Degree Certificate. Do not authenticate documents that the recipient has not requested.

Transcript Apostille Review Checklist

Practical review guidance—not a universal official document list:

  • Issuing university and graduation year.
  • Transcript type and current form.
  • Original, copy or scan status.
  • Chinese-only or bilingual format and page count, where relevant.
  • Destination country, receiving organization and intended use.
  • Exact recipient instructions.
  • Apostille, CHSI/CSSD, WES or direct-submission request.
  • Existing notarization and deadline.

Common Mistakes

  • Arranging Apostille before confirming it is required.
  • Assuming the university original goes directly to Apostille.
  • Treating CHSI/CSSD verification as Apostille.
  • Assuming WES submission and Apostille are the same process.
  • Assuming every sealed transcript must remain sealed for notarization.
  • Inventing universal page-stamp or riding-seal rules.
  • Assuming bilingual content removes every translation requirement.
  • Expecting Apostille to verify grades or equivalency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a Chinese Academic Transcript need an Apostille?

Only if the destination route and receiving organization require it. Confirm the request before arranging authentication.

Does a Chinese transcript need notarization before Apostille?

Yes, for a transcript issued by a Chinese educational institution covered by current China MFA guidance. It must first undergo notarization in China, after which the eligible foreign-related notarial certificate may proceed to Apostille where required.

Can the original university transcript be submitted directly for Apostille?

Current MFA guidance requires the listed educational records to undergo notarization first. The original may serve as source or supporting evidence, while the eligible resulting notarial document proceeds to Apostille.

Can a photocopy, scan or system printout be Apostilled?

Do not assume so. These forms do not automatically establish official status or eligibility. The notary and relevant authorities must confirm the acceptable evidence and resulting document form.

Does a transcript need to be in a sealed envelope for Apostille?

There is no universal Apostille rule requiring a sealed envelope. Sealing may instead be required by WES, a university or another recipient.

Does every transcript page need a university stamp or riding seal?

No universal national rule should be assumed. Confirm completeness, seals, binding and attachment requirements with the university, notary and relevant authority.

Is CHSI/CSSD transcript verification the same as Apostille?

No. CHSI/CSSD verification assesses academic information through its own service. Apostille authenticates formal origin elements of a qualifying public or notarial document.

Does WES require an Apostilled transcript?

Do not assume that it does. Follow WES's current country- and credential-specific Required Documents instructions for the particular evaluation.

Is direct university submission the same as Apostille?

No. Direct submission is a delivery or verification mechanism controlled by the recipient. Apostille is a formal authentication under the applicable cross-border route.

Does Apostille verify grades, GPA or academic equivalency?

No. It does not verify grades or GPA, determine academic equivalency or guarantee recipient acceptance.
