Overview

Do You Need a China PCC for Spain?

Some Spanish visa, residence and administrative procedures require criminal-record evidence covering previous countries of residence. Whether China is included depends on your history and the specific route. A short-stay visa, a residence visa and a residence authorization are not the same process, and a China PCC is not mandatory for every Spanish application.

Under Spain’s current immigration regulation, the residence-visa rules include a criminal-record condition for applicants above the age of criminal responsibility concerning countries of residence during the preceding five years. Non-lucrative and relevant work-residence routes must be checked against their own procedure.

International teleworkers follow a distinct framework. The official UGE guidance distinguishes certificates for the previous two years of residence from a five-year criminal-record declaration; applicable exceptions and the submission route must also be checked. Do not treat these periods as a universal PCC validity rule.

Send the consular or Spanish authority’s current checklist, residence history and appointment date before ordering a China-side document chain. Other EU countries have their own rules. Document Preparation

Required Documents: Start With Your China Records

This is a preparation guide, not a nationwide mandatory checklist. Requirements depend on nationality, city, residence period and the local authority’s review.

Identity & Travel History

  • Current passport bio page.
  • Former passport used during your stay in China, if available.
  • Chinese visas, entry/exit stamps and residence permits, as applicable.
  • Evidence explaining a name change or passport-number mismatch.

Residence & Authorization

  • Temporary Residence Registration Form, where applicable.
  • Dates and addresses in each Chinese city.
  • Work or school records that may help locate archives.
  • Power of attorney or other authorization, where permitted.
  • Photos or fingerprints only if the local procedure requests them.

This is not a nationwide mandatory list. Chinese citizens may also need identity, hukou and local records; foreign applicants follow the relevant local procedure. Bring the Spanish request letter and any appointment or submission deadline to the route review. Additional forms, photographs or fingerprints are needed only where the local authority requests them. We cannot guarantee record recovery or certificate issuance.

Applying From Outside China

How to Get a China PCC for Spain After Leaving China

Confirm the Responsible City and Route

Leaving China does not automatically rule out an application. Send your former Chinese cities, residence dates, current country, passport history and available records. An online route or authorized representative may be possible only where the responsible local authority permits it.

Old passports, visas, residence permits and temporary residence registration can affect eligibility and record checks. A visitor visa and a residence permit are different records. Some routes require personal attendance, additional identity checks or a locally specified Power of Attorney.

Read the guide to applying after leaving China.

Plan Around Missing Records and Deadlines

For lost passports, missing registration, several Chinese cities or inconsistent names, first check which evidence can be verified locally. Archived or reissued records may be explored where the procedure allows; availability is not assured. School or employment records are not automatically accepted replacements.

Send the Spanish recipient’s exact checklist and appointment date before preparing authorization, translation or delivery. If the certificate cannot be issued, ask that recipient what explanation or alternative evidence it will consider. There is no verified universal Spanish substitute or automatic waiver.

Spain Document Format

Does a China Police Certificate for Spain Need Spanish Translation or an Apostille?

Plan the China police record, notarial instrument, accepted translation and authentication separately. The consular filing route and an application submitted inside Spain can use different document instructions.

Spanish Translation

Check whether the recipient requires an official Spanish translation, including Traducción Jurada. If a sworn translator is needed, verify the language authorization in the Spanish ministry’s translator directory. Ordinary translation and an English version are not automatic substitutes.

The immigration authority’s translation guidance recognizes specified official routes, with their own formalities. A Chinese notarial document incorporating Spanish should not be assumed to meet every office’s requirements. We help plan the requested format; we do not claim unverified sworn-translator credentials.

Notarization

A China PCC issued by the responsible public security authority and a Chinese notarial certificate (公证书) are different instruments. The notarial act and its supporting documents must match the intended use.

The Spanish Consulate in Shanghai’s China-document guidance describes a Chinese notarial instrument incorporating Spanish, followed by apostille. Confirm that route with the actual recipient; do not automatically order a second translation or assume that consular guidance settles every inland residence procedure.

Apostille

Since 7 November 2023, covered mainland Chinese public documents for Spain use the apostille route instead of Spanish consular legalization. The Chinese Embassy in Spain identifies China’s MFA and authorized local foreign affairs offices as competent Chinese authorities.

An apostille is not a translation. It concerns the document’s origin-related formalities, not proof of its contents or guaranteed acceptance. Confirm the specific public instrument, any exemption and the recipient’s requirements. Do not treat apostille and consular legalization as two routine consecutive steps.

Ask which pages, notarial attachments and apostille need translation before commissioning the work. View China PCC format samples and document notarization and apostille assistance. This is China-side preparation support, not a promise of visa approval or acceptance by all Spanish or EU authorities.

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a China Police Certificate for a Spanish visa?

The answer depends on the application route, age, residence history and consulate’s checklist. Residence visas under Spain’s immigration regulation have a criminal-record condition that is not the same as a universal short-stay visa requirement. International teleworker applications use a separate framework. Confirm whether your China residence falls within the relevant period and which document the actual recipient requires.

Can I obtain a China PCC after leaving China?

There may still be a local route. We review your former Chinese cities, residence dates, passport history and available records before preparing authorization. A representative or online application is possible only where the responsible authority permits it; some procedures require personal attendance or additional checks. Remote issuance is not guaranteed in every city.

What documents are required?

The preparation review may include current and former passports, Chinese visas, residence permits where applicable, temporary residence registration, dates and addresses, and the Spanish checklist. Name-change evidence, school or employment records, a Power of Attorney, local forms or fingerprints may be relevant. The issuing Chinese authority determines the city-specific list.

Can I apply without a Chinese residence permit?

A residence permit is different from a visitor visa. Whether your stay and evidence qualify must be checked with the responsible Chinese authority. Provide the records you have, including your visa and registration history, before arranging replacements or travel. We do not promise eligibility or successful issuance in every case.

What if I have lost my previous passport?

Send any copy, old passport number, available visas and records connecting your former identity to the current passport. The local office may ask for additional evidence or checks. Registration, work or school records can assist only where accepted; they are not guaranteed substitutes for a missing passport.

Does Spain require a Spanish translation?

Many procedures require an accepted official Spanish translation. Check whether Traducción Jurada or another specifically recognized route applies. A Chinese ordinary translation or an English version is not automatically sufficient. Consular guidance for Chinese notarial instruments incorporating Spanish must be checked separately from inland immigration rules. Confirm the translator, complete pages and apostille coverage with the actual receiving office.

Does my China PCC need notarization?

The police-issued record and a Chinese notarial certificate are different documents. The Spanish Consulate in Shanghai describes a China-document route using a notarial instrument incorporating Spanish before apostille. Confirm the requested instrument with your recipient and the competent Chinese office. Do not assume every Spanish application follows an identical notarial or translation process.

Do I need an apostille for Spain?

For covered mainland Chinese public documents submitted in Spain, the Apostille Convention route has applied since 7 November 2023, replacing Spanish consular legalization. Check the particular instrument, any exemption and the recipient’s checklist. An apostille does not translate the document, certify its underlying contents or guarantee acceptance; do not automatically add a consular legalization step.

How long does it take to obtain a China PCC?

Timing depends on the Chinese city, historical records and local authority review. Missing documents, several cities or overseas authorization can add work. Any required Spanish translation, notarization, apostille and international delivery take additional time. We give a case-specific estimate after review, without a nationwide completion promise.

What if the Chinese authorities cannot issue my certificate?

Keep evidence of the application attempts and any written response. Ask the Spanish recipient what explanation or alternative evidence its particular procedure allows. There is no verified universal substitute or automatic waiver for every Spanish visa or residence route. Do not assume that a personal declaration replaces the required China PCC unless that authority explicitly permits it.
