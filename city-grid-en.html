Overview

A China Police Certificate may be requested for immigration, employment, study, residence, licensing, or other background-check purposes. Application procedures and supporting documents can vary between Chinese cities because local authorities may apply different requirements.

If you lived in more than one Chinese city, review your residence history and the period requested by the receiving authority before choosing the appropriate application route.

Common Questions About City Applications

Does the application process differ by city?

Yes. Local authorities may use different accepting offices, residence evidence and document-handling procedures. Confirm the current requirements for the Chinese city, district and period relevant to your application; these practical guides do not replace official instructions.

What documents are usually required?

Possible materials include a current or previous passport, Chinese visas or residence permits, temporary residence registration, address history, school or employment records and authorization documents. The exact list depends on the city and your circumstances; this is not a nationwide mandatory checklist.

Can I apply if I have lived in multiple cities?

Start with the cities where you lived, studied or worked during the period requested by the receiving authority. Review each residence period and available records before deciding whether one route or several local applications are appropriate.

Can I apply after leaving China?

An application route may still be available, depending on the city, your residence history, supporting records and local authorization rules. Some authorities allow a representative; others may require attendance or additional checks. Remote processing and issuance cannot be guaranteed in every case.

Do I need notarization or an apostille for use abroad?

Ask the actual receiving authority which document form, language and authentication it requires. A police-issued record, a Chinese notarial certificate, translation, apostille and consular legalization are different instruments or procedures. Not every application needs each step, and an apostille does not guarantee acceptance.

How long does it usually take?

Processing time varies by Chinese city, document availability and the local authority’s review. Missing records, several residence locations, authorization, translation, notarization, authentication and delivery can add time. Request a case-specific estimate rather than relying on a fixed nationwide timeline.
