CHSI Verification Report Expired? How to Extend and Send It Overseas

A practical guide to identifying an expired CHSI or CSSD report, extending online verification where available, and choosing the correct overseas submission route.

By Global Attest Document Services Team · Published: September 7, 2026
Direct answer: If the online verification period shown on a CHSI report has expired, this usually means that the online verification access has ended—not that your degree, qualification or student record has become invalid. Your next step depends on the exact report type. CHSI expressly provides an extension route for three online verification reports, while formal CSSD verification reports and transcript reports may be handled differently. The overseas recipient also decides whether it will accept an extended report, a new report or official digital delivery.

An old PDF can still display your education information, but its verification code, QR code or web link may no longer return an active result. Before extending or reapplying, identify what you have and read the current instructions from the university, employer, immigration authority, licensing body or credential evaluator receiving it.

What Does “Expired” Mean on a CHSI or CSSD Report?

Several different dates and functions can be confused:

  • The academic record: the qualification, degree or period of study recorded by the university and relevant education system.
  • The PDF: the copy you downloaded. Keeping the file does not guarantee that its online verification remains active.
  • The online verification period: the period during which the official code or link can be checked online.
  • The QR code: a route to the official verification service; it may not produce an active result after the applicable verification period.
  • The recipient’s recency rule: an overseas organization may independently require a report issued, verified or delivered within a stated period.

The CHSI online verification instructions distinguish the report’s verification validity from the education information it displays. Extending the former should not be described as extending the validity of a degree.

CHSI/CSSD does not use one single “CHSI report” for every purpose. The official service distinguishes online verification reports from formal verification reports. Start with the title printed on the document and the service through which it was issued.

Can an Expired CHSI Report Be Extended?

CHSI’s current official guidance expressly identifies three reports that can use the online extension function: the Online Verification Report of Student Record, Online Verification Report of Higher Education Qualification Certificate and Online Verification Report of Higher Education Degree Certificate.

The Chinese guidance says a holder may extend within one month before expiry or after the report has expired. The official CHSI extension page directs the user to CHSI Archive and the issued online report.

This should not be generalized to every CSSD product. CHSI’s official service overview separates online reports from formal qualification, degree and transcript verification. The public extension instructions reviewed do not confirm that every formal report uses the same extension function.

Confirmed: the three named online verification reports have an official extension route. Not confirmed as the same process: formal qualification verification, formal degree verification, transcript verification and every English or translated output.

How to Extend the Online Verification Period

For one of the supported online reports, the official route is:

  1. Sign in to the correct CHSI Archive account. Use the account that contains the education record and issued report.
  2. Open Online Verification Reports. Confirm that the report shown is the same type and number as your saved copy.
  3. Check the current status. Review the report title, verification code and displayed validity.
  4. Use the extension function offered for that report. CHSI’s Chinese guidance labels this function “延长验证有效期”.
  5. Select from the options currently displayed. Availability, permitted period and any charge should be taken from the live account rather than an old screenshot.
  6. Complete the platform steps. Do not share passwords, SMS codes or real-name verification credentials with a third party.
  7. Re-open the report. Test the online verification result and QR code, and download the current output if the recipient permits a PDF.
  8. Keep evidence of submission. Save the current report and the recipient’s instructions.

The CHSI English verification page says extension is currently accessible to CHSI Archive users. If you cannot access the account, solve the account or identity issue through the official platform; do not create inconsistent records merely to obtain another login.

Does Extending the Report Change the Report Number?

The official public material reviewed does not provide one reliable answer covering every report, historical format and English output. Do not assume that all identifiers remain unchanged or that a saved PDF updates itself.

Check these details after extension

  • report title and report number;
  • issue date and verification-expiry date;
  • online verification code;
  • QR code result;
  • online verification URL;
  • name, passport or identification details; and
  • language and delivery format requested by the recipient.

If the current output differs from a document already submitted, follow the recipient’s replacement-document or update instructions.

How to Send a CHSI Report Overseas

An active report can still be sent through the wrong channel. Overseas organizations may ask for an online link, a QR-verifiable PDF, applicant upload or official electronic delivery. A downloaded PDF is not automatically sufficient for every recipient.

CHSI’s official digital-transmission guidance describes delivery of applicable CSSD verification reports to supported partner organizations. That is distinct from emailing a PDF yourself. Before sending, confirm:

  • the exact report type and language;
  • whether active online verification is required;
  • whether the applicant may upload the document;
  • whether CHSI/CSSD must transmit it through an official channel; and
  • whether the recipient imposes an issue-date or recency rule beyond CHSI validity.

For a broader map of report types and delivery roles, see the CHSI and CSSD academic verification reports resource center.

Does WES Accept an Extended CHSI or CSSD Report?

There is no universal yes. WES controls the Required Documents and submission route for the applicant’s education history and evaluation. An extended online verification period does not automatically satisfy a requirement for CSSD to send a particular report.

Start with the WES Required Documents tool and the current instructions in the applicant’s account. If WES requires CSSD delivery, use the official route rather than assuming an uploaded PDF or QR code is enough.

Global Attest’s WES credential evaluation support page explains document coordination, while the China-to-WES preparation guide covers the wider process. If a file was rejected, review why WES may reject Chinese academic documents. Extending a report cannot guarantee WES acceptance or evaluation outcome.

What If the Report Cannot Be Extended?

Use a diagnosis sequence instead of repeatedly pressing the same option:

  1. Confirm that the document is one of the three supported online report types.
  2. Confirm that you are signed into the CHSI Archive account containing that report.
  3. Check whether the document belongs to a different formal CHSI/CSSD verification service.
  4. Resolve historical account, mobile-number, email or identity-access problems through official support.
  5. Check whether the service requires a new application rather than an extension.
  6. Contact CHSI/CSSD official support for a platform, report or delivery problem.
  7. Contact the university only when the underlying education record or archive data requires correction or confirmation.
  8. Ask the overseas recipient whether it requires a new report, active verification or another delivery method.

For example, CSSD’s formal verification application guidance and its transcript verification report guidance describe services separate from the three online reports named on the extension page. No identical extension button should be promised for those reports without checking the current service.

Old Passport, Changed Name or Account Problems

International graduates may find that CHSI or university data uses the passport number and name held during study. A renewed passport does not automatically replace that historical identity information.

Keep your current passport and any available old passport number, scan, Chinese visa, residence permit, certificate copy and name-change evidence. CHSI’s guidance for international graduates emphasizes matching the study-period passport number and the name recorded on the graduation certificate, including letter case, in its registration-and-query context. This does not make an old passport a universal requirement for every report extension; the platform decides what evidence is required for account or record matching.

Do not share login credentials or verification codes with a service provider. Use the detailed CHSI old-passport and name-mismatch troubleshooting guide. If the degree itself cannot be found, the separate guide on a Chinese degree not found on CHSI addresses source-record diagnosis.

When Should You Contact the Chinese University?

A report’s online expiry normally points first to the report platform, not to an error in the university’s academic record. Contact the university when the source record is missing, incorrect or requires archive confirmation—not merely because an online verification period ended.

  • University: source academic data, archives, transcripts and university-issued documents.
  • CHSI/CSSD: account access, report application, verification status and supported delivery services.
  • Overseas recipient: accepted report type, language, recency and submission channel.

If the required transcript is missing, review how to obtain an old Chinese university transcript after graduation. Do not ask the university to alter a correct record simply to refresh an expired CHSI link.

Step-by-Step Decision Checklist

  1. Identify the report. Copy its exact printed title and note which official service issued it.
  2. Check what expired. Separate online verification validity from the education record and the recipient’s own recency rule.
  3. Read the recipient’s current requirements. Confirm report type, language and sender.
  4. Check for the official extension route. Use CHSI Archive for the three named online reports.
  5. Extend or reapply through the correct service. Do not assume formal reports use the same route.
  6. Verify the refreshed details. Test the code, QR and URL and confirm identity data.
  7. Send through the required channel. Use applicant upload, online verification or official digital delivery as specified.
  8. Keep proof of submission. Save the report version and the recipient’s instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a CHSI report expire?

Some CHSI online verification reports display a verification validity period. When that period ends, online verification may stop until the period is extended where the official service allows it. This does not by itself invalidate the underlying education record.

Does an expired report mean my degree is invalid?

No. An expired online verification period concerns access to verification, not the academic award itself. A separate data error or university-record problem must be diagnosed on its own facts.

Can every CHSI or CSSD report be extended?

No universal extension rule is published for every report. CHSI expressly describes extension for three online verification reports. Formal qualification, degree and transcript verification reports may use different services or require a new application.

Where can I extend the verification period?

For the supported online reports, CHSI directs users to sign in to CHSI Archive, open Online Verification Reports, locate the issued report and use the extension function shown for that report.

Do I need the original CHSI account?

The official extension route is available through CHSI Archive, so access to the account containing the report is normally important. If the report is not visible, resolve the account or identity issue through the official platform rather than creating duplicate records.

Does extension change the report number?

The official public guidance reviewed does not establish one answer for every report or output. After extending, check the report number, issue and validity dates, online verification code, QR code and verification URL before sending it.

Can I send the extended PDF to an overseas university?

Only if the university accepts applicant-uploaded PDFs for that requirement. Another recipient may require an active verification link, QR check, official digital delivery or a newly issued report.

Does WES accept an extended CHSI report?

There is no automatic guarantee. Follow the Required Documents and delivery instructions in the applicant’s current WES account. Extending online access does not replace a required CSSD delivery route.

What if the QR code no longer works?

Check the report’s validity date and online verification status. If it is a supported online report, use the official extension route and test the refreshed report. Otherwise use the report-specific support or application route.

What if I changed my passport?

A changed passport may affect account or record matching, especially for international graduates. Keep the current passport and available study-period passport details, and use CHSI’s official identity-support route if the report cannot be accessed.

What if the report cannot be extended?

Confirm the report type, account and official service first. You may need to reapply through the correct CHSI/CSSD service or contact official support. Contact the university only if the source academic record needs attention.

Should I contact CHSI, CSSD or my university?

Use CHSI/CSSD support for account, platform, report or delivery issues; contact the university for source-record or archive issues; and ask the overseas recipient about acceptance, recency and submission requirements.

Can Global Attest extend the report for me?

Global Attest can help identify the report and explain the route, but cannot enter or control a client’s personal account, impersonate the applicant, bypass real-name verification or change official records.

How long should I extend it for?

Choose an available period that covers the recipient’s review and expected follow-up, but use the options shown in the current official account. Recipient recency rules and platform limits may differ.

Need Help With an Expired CHSI or CSSD Report?

For an initial review, provide the report type, Chinese university, graduation year, receiving organization, current report status or error message, whether the original account is accessible, current passport status and deadline.

A complete passport scan is not required during the first public enquiry. If the case can proceed, necessary identity or academic evidence can be requested through an appropriate private channel.
