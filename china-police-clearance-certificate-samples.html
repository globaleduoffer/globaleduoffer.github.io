China Police Clearance Certificate Samples

Reference samples for China police clearance certificates, China police certificates, the China No Criminal Record Certificate guide and China PCC documents.

Overview

This resource shows redacted examples of China Police Clearance Certificates and related notarized document bundles prepared for overseas use.

The exact document format and notarization route may vary depending on the issuing authority, destination country, notary office and receiving organization.

Sample documents are provided for reference only. Personal names, passport or identity numbers and QR codes have been redacted for privacy.
