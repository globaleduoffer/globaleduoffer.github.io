How to Read a China PCC

A China Police Clearance Certificate is issued by the relevant public security authority and records the result of a criminal-record check for the period or scope covered by the certificate.

  1. 1

    Name

    The applicant’s name as recorded on the certificate.

  2. 2

    ID / Passport Number

    The identity document number shown on the certificate, where applicable.

  3. 3

    Checked Period

    The period covered by the certificate, if specified.

  4. 4

    Result

    The criminal-record check result stated by the issuing authority.

Key Takeaways

  • A China PCC is issued by the relevant public security authority.
  • It records the result of a criminal-record check.
  • The format and information shown may vary by city.
  • Translation or notarization requirements depend on the receiving authority.
  • Verify the name, identity information and covered period where applicable.

This sample specifies a checked period. Other documents may use different wording or omit particular fields. Any validity wording printed on this individual sample must not be treated as a rule for every city or receiving authority.

Common Questions (FAQ)

Can I apply for a China PCC after leaving China?

You may be able to apply through an authorized representative or another procedure accepted by the local issuing authority. Eligibility, authorization documents and the available route depend on the Chinese city and your records. A remote application is not guaranteed in every case.

Do I need an English translation and notarization?

Check the receiving authority’s instructions first. It may request a translation, a notarial document set or further authentication, depending on the purpose and destination. These are separate steps; they are not automatically required for every application.

Will the PCC format be the same in every city?

No. Wording, layout, fields and the period or scope covered may differ by issuing authority and date. The examples on this page show individual cases, not a mandatory national template.

What if I don’t have a residence registration record?

A missing record does not by itself establish whether you can obtain a certificate. The local authority must assess eligibility and the evidence available for your stay. Ask whether other supporting records are accepted; incomplete evidence may affect the route or period that can be checked.
